From Our Farm to Your Family

Shri Garud Agro Corporation, established in 2023 with a simple mission: bring pure, unprocessed honey and natural wellness food to Indian households.

We partner with local beekeepsers supported farms spanning acres of rich, unpolluted farmland of Pratapgarh, Uttar Pradesh. Every drop of our honey tells the story of nature's finest work — untouched, unfiltered, and absolutely pure.

The Founder

Meet Sudhanshu Tripathi

"Natural" is the most misused word in the Indian food industry. Any brand can print it on a label without proof. I started UNT Naturals because I wanted to create something genuinely different — honey that people can trust completely, where every jar is traceable back to the hive it came from."

As the founder and director of Shri Garud Agro Corporation, Sudhanshu works directly with beekeepers to ensure every batch meets the highest standards, bringing hyperlocal, raw honey's goodness to tables across India.
